We'd rather ask than assume.

Technology is often built around assumptions — about what a business needs, how it operates, what would make it easier.

Business Pulse exists to ask Nigerian businesses directly what's difficult, inefficient, expensive, frustrating, or unnecessarily manual — instead of guessing.

IGRIS Technologies builds websites, software, AI solutions, and automation. But the research comes first. IGRIS services are secondary.
